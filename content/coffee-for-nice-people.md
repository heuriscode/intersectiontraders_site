# Coffee for nice people
*By Daniel Gregg · 15 Sep 2025 · archived from intersectiontraders.com.au/posts/coffee-for-nice-people*

You like coffee as much for its flavour as for the caffeine it endows you with. You like people, and want to do what's best for others but without impoverishing yourself. You want to spend time understanding where your food comes from but you don't have the time to do this for everything.

You're a nice person. You want nice coffee. That's our jam. We deal with the complex stuff, you drink great coffee. You trust we do what we say because we have evidence and are leaders in the technical stuff (i.e. research). You tell your friends and family. We're all happy, nice, caffeinated people enjoying good coffee that does good things.

If you're into procrastination then dive deep on topics related to our coffee, ethics, systems, theory, innovation, and evidence: Our coffee · Ethics – concepts and our ethics · Science, innovation, and evidence · Value, impact, and sustainability.
